Terms of Use

Draft for student review. Have your instructor or adviser check this before launch.

These terms cover your use of the Leyte Tourism Guide, a student capstone project that helps visitors discover and plan trips to selected destinations in Leyte Province.

1. What this system is for

Leyte Tourism Guide provides information about selected tourist destinations in Leyte, including descriptions, entrance fees, operating hours, and facilities. It offers point-to-point route estimates through the Google Maps Directions API and current weather conditions through the OpenWeather API, along with a suggested one-day itinerary for a destination you choose.

2. What this system does not do

  • It does not process payments, bookings, or reservations of any kind (hotels, tours, transportation, or tickets).
  • It does not provide specific public transportation routes, such as particular jeepney, van, or tricycle lines.
  • It only covers selected destinations within Leyte Province, not the whole province or destinations elsewhere.

3. Accuracy of information

Destination details are entered and maintained by the system administrator and are not automatically verified against every tourist establishment. Route distances and travel times come from the Google Maps Directions API and can change with traffic and map data. Weather conditions come from the OpenWeather API and may vary in accuracy and availability. Confirm details directly with a destination before you travel.

4. Your account

You're responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide when you register, and for keeping your password private. You can update your profile or delete your account at any time from your dashboard.

5. Acceptable use

Use the system as intended: to browse destinations, plan routes, and share honest ratings and reviews. Don't submit false reviews, attempt to access other users' accounts, or misuse the destination data.

6. Internet connection required

Map routing and weather features need an active internet connection to work.

7. Changes to these terms

As a student project, these terms may change as the system develops. Continued use after a change means you accept the update.
