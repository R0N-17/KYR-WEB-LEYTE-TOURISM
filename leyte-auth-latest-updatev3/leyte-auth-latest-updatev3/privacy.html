Privacy Notice

Draft for student review. Have your instructor or adviser check this before launch.

This notice explains what information the Leyte Tourism Guide collects and how it's used.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored as a hash, never in plain text).
  • Preferences: the destination types, setting, time of day, budget, and trip length you choose in the preference questionnaire.
  • Favorites: the destinations you save for quick access.
  • Ratings and reviews: anything you submit about a destination.
  • History logs: destinations you've viewed and routes you've planned, kept within your account.

We don't collect payment information, since the system doesn't process bookings or payments.

2. How it's used

Your information is used only to run the features you use directly: signing you in, showing your saved preferences and favorites, recommending destinations, and keeping your history for your own future reference. It isn't sold, and it isn't used to advertise to you.

3. Information shared with outside services

When you plan a route, your starting location and chosen destination are sent to the Google Maps Directions API to calculate the route, distance, and travel time. When you check the weather for a destination, that destination's location is sent to the OpenWeather API. Neither service receives your account details.

4. Who can see your data

Your account information, favorites, ratings, reviews, and history are accessible only through your own registered account. The administrator manages destination records (descriptions, fees, hours, facilities, images) but does not need your personal data to do so.

5. Your choices

You can update your profile, change your travel preferences, or delete your account at any time from your dashboard. Deleting your account removes your profile, favorites, and history.

6. Changes to this notice

As a student project, this notice may change as the system develops.
